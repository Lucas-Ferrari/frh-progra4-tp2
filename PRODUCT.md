# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary audience: the professors grading this Programación IV integrator project. They review it on XAMPP or the Railway deploy, walk each role's flows, and check that features work and read clearly.

The site simulates a fictional bank, Future Bank, with four roles: visitors (bank info, sign-up, contact), clientes (accounts, movements with search, transfers, editing their email), operadores (create clients, search clients, record deposits and withdrawals), and administradores (summary panel and CRUD for accounts, movements, users and enquiries).

## Product Purpose
To show a complete, working banking site in PHP + MySQL + HTML/CSS/JS with no frameworks. Success means a grader can find and finish every role's flow without confusion, and the interface looks deliberate and coherent.

## Constraints
- Restyle only: keep the existing pages, forms, fields and flows. No structural or flow changes.
- Stack is fixed: PHP, MySQL, plain HTML/CSS/JavaScript.
- Fictional bank for an academic project; the footer says so. No invented claims, rates or testimonials.
- Spanish (Argentina) UI copy.
- The earlier "Aguayo" woven-cloth redesign (branch feature/redesign) was rejected as too themed, gimmicky and unprofessional. Don't reuse its direction.

## Brand Commitments
- Clean, sleek, modern bank/fintech look. No themed or cultural-craft concepts.
- A concise, coherent color palette.
- Standing preference: the category-standard neo-bank look, played straight. Reference bar: Ualá, Brubank, Lemon, Nubank.
- Content reveals itself as you scroll (fade/slide-in as sections enter the viewport); respect reduced motion.
